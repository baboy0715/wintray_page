Key Features

  • ❄ Winter & Autumn Season Modes: Seamlessly switch between winter snowfall and cozy autumn atmosphere from the system tray menu, featuring automated desktop wallpaper switching.
  • ❄ Realistic Leaf Fall & Autumn Rain: 3 distinct leaf types (birch, oak, maple) across 4 autumn color tones, accompanied by gentle slanted raindrops and ripple splash effects.
  • ❄ Realistic 120 FPS Desktop Snow: Multiple snowflake styles, wind physics, optional snow thickness limit removal, and growing icicles that shatter on click.
  • ❄ Physical Window Accumulation & Cursor Sweep: Snow and leaves settle realistically on top of open window frames and the taskbar, scattering when windows move or swept with your mouse cursor.
  • ❄ Multi-language & 0% Background Gaming Impact: English and Hungarian system tray settings, smooth 120 FPS fade-outs, and automatic suspension during full-screen apps and games.
