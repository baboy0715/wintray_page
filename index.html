Frequently Asked Questions

What makes WinterTray the best winter theme for Windows 10 & 11?

WinterTray transforms your desktop with realistic 120 FPS falling snow, seasonal wallpapers, and interactive physics. Unlike static themes, WinterTray brings your desktop to life with customizable wind, snowflake styles, and autumn atmosphere.

How does real-time window snow accumulation work?

WinterTray tracks active desktop windows and taskbars in real time. Snowflakes and falling leaves land and settle on window frames. When you move an application or sweep your mouse cursor across them, the accumulated snow crumbles realistically.

Does this desktop snow app impact gaming or PC performance?

Not at all! WinterTray is hardware-accelerated with near-zero CPU and RAM footprint. In addition, it features automatic smart suspension when full-screen 3D games or productivity apps are running.

Is WinterTray free and safe to download?

Yes! WinterTray is completely free, lightweight, and contains zero ads, telemetry, or bloatware. Download the official installer (.exe) directly from our releases section.
